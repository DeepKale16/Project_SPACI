Space-Debris Autonomous Capture Demonstrator

Catch what's left behind.

SPACI is an autonomous space-debris detection, aiming and net-capture machine. It detects moving debris, computes lead intercept angles, and captures targets in a tethered Dyneema net. Built and proven first as a ground demonstrator.

Perception & Tracking

LiDAR ranging and camera vision locate the tumbling target.

Monocular cameras classify debris taxonomy while pulsed LiDAR depth returns provide metric range. A pure-NumPy 6-state 3D Kalman filter tracks position and velocity vectors while filtering spurious noise.

Ballistic Intercept & Launch

Four ejectors deploy the tethered net.

A closed-form quartic ballistic solver computes gimbal slew angles and compensation. Four high-pressure pneumatic ejector tubes fire corner projectile slugs outward, deploying a 2.0m x 2.0m high-tensile net across the deployment corridor.

Pan-Tilt Gimbal 4 Ejector Barrels Magnetic Slugs Dyneema Mesh

Permanent N52 neodymium magnets on corner slugs assist contact adhesion when latching onto ferrous fragments.

Drawstring Cinch & Winch

Close the net. Bring it in.

Upon wrapping the tumbling debris, a motorized drawstring cinches the mouth of the net shut, locking the target inside. The motorized winch spool reels the tether and captured debris back to the capture machine chassis.

Drawstring Ring Motorized Winch Tether Spool

Kinematic momentum transfer is damped through tension regulation, preventing recoil against the capture vehicle.

System Architecture

What SPACI is made of.

DetectLiDAR & Optical Cameras: Monocular 640x640 vision combined with pulsed time-of-flight depth adapter (10 Hz, 0.05 m accuracy).
AimPan-Tilt Mechanism: Dual-axis stepper actuation (-15° to +45° elevation, ±75° azimuth) slewing at 35°/s via closed-form quartic ballistic intercept solver.
CaptureFour Pneumatic Ejectors & Net: 6-8 bar solenoid launch barrels deploying corner slugs with N52 neodymium magnetic assists and 2m x 2m high-tensile nylon/Dyneema mesh.
RetrieveDrawstring & Winch: Perimeter cinching ring actuated by geared DC motor drum spool with active tension damping.
Test DebrisSimulated Moving Targets: Standardized representative test pieces varying in geometry, weight (0.2 - 2.5 kg), and materials (aluminum, polymers, ferrous alloys).
MeasuredStandardized KPIs: Object detection mAP (%), ballistic miss distance error (m), and physical capture success rate (% of trials).

The AI & Vision Pipeline

From raw photon stream to lead aim point.

01 Sense

LiDAR range returns and camera frames synchronize with sub-millisecond timestamps.

02 Detect

Deep neural network classifies debris taxonomy across 11 visual classes.

03 Locate

Outlier rejection (MAD/IQR) isolates target centroid 3D spatial coordinates.

04 Predict

6-state pure-NumPy 3D Kalman filter estimates velocity and tumbling variance.

05 Aim

Quartic polynomial solver calculates lead intercept vector compensating for actuation latency.

Test Strategy

Why test on the ground first.

Ground Demonstrator

  • Built and operated inside a laboratory capture cage.
  • Uses physical test debris moving along rail and suspension rigs.
  • Compensates for 1-G terrestrial gravity in the ballistic trajectory solver.
  • Enables high-frequency repeatable trials to prove sensors, net aperture, and solenoid timing.
  • Zero orbital risk, low operational budget, and rapid firmware iteration cycles.

Orbital Concept (Future Spacecraft)

  • Free-flying spacecraft bus operating in microgravity environment.
  • Calculates zero-G parabolic intercept trajectories without aerodynamic drag.
  • Utilizes cold-gas or RCS thrusters for proximity operations and rendezvous.
  • Radiation-hardened avionics and space-qualified solar panels.
  • De-orbits captured debris via controlled atmospheric re-entry burn.

Multidisciplinary Subsystems

Engineered across four core aerospace disciplines.

Mechanical

Pan-tilt gimbal, 4-barrel pneumatic launcher, high-pressure accumulator manifold, Dyneema net, and motorized winch spool.

Electronics

24V/12V dual-rail power distribution, solid-state solenoid drivers, optical encoder feedback, and isolated emergency cut-off bus.

Embedded Control

Microcontroller executing gimbal slewing, pressure monitoring, safety gating, and telemetry serialization at sub-millisecond loop intervals.

AI & Vision

Neural detector, pulsed time-of-flight LiDAR integration, pure-NumPy 3D Kalman filter, and quartic ballistic intercept solver.

Safety, Ethics & Aerospace Education

A safe, transparent platform to study space technology.

Active debris removal involves complex orbital dynamics: unregulated impulses risk triggering catastrophic breakups under NASA-STD-8719.14C criteria (energy exceeding 40 J/g). SPACI is an open research and educational demonstrator that allows aerospace researchers and students to study autonomous computer vision, safety-interlocked GNC, and active capture mechanics safely on Earth before committing flight hardware.

Earth imagery: NASA Visible Earth Blue Marble, night lights, and water specular mask via three-globe.
